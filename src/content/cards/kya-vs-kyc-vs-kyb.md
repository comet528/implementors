Know Your Agent sits beside Know Your Customer and Know Your Business; it does not replace them. Keep the layers distinct: customer due diligence, business due diligence, and whatever agent-identity checks you add for software that acts. Collapsing them into one check is how liability and auth evidence go fuzzy.
