Public agentic-banking framing from Anchorage puts mandate and policy checks at the regulated account layer—before settlement, on rails the model cannot rewrite. An agent that can be steered by a prompt is not a substitute for account-side enforcement; identity and mandate belong with the custodian, not inside the agent.
