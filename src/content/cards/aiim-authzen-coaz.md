The OpenID AIIM Community Group is the public venue for agent-identity and delegated-authority gaps. AuthZEN standardises PEP↔PDP authorization exchange; CoAZ maps source models into AuthZEN, including an MCP binding for tool calls. Design them as separate layers: who the agent is, then what it is allowed to invoke.
