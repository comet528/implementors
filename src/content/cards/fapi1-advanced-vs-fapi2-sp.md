FAPI is the OpenID Foundation’s security-profile family for financial-grade APIs. UK Open Banking v4 still aligns to **FAPI 1 Advanced**; the FAPI 2 Security Profile is a separate published instrument. When you wire conformance or read a bank’s profile, treat FAPI 2 Baseline as a different path—not the UK rulebook.
